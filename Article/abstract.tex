\textbf{Abstract}\\
Tiny Tapeout is a recently established Open Source Silicon platform. 
Their system relies on taking things beyond multi-project wafers to include multiple projects on individual pieces of silicon. 
Once a design is fabricated it is typically returned to the customer alongside a carrier board intended to simplify interacting with the project. 
The carrier board presents a set of three PMOD headers for the silicon projects inputs, outputs, and bidirection io. 
This work presents a custom PMOD board based on this setup intended to allow for more easily capturing and even modifying these singals. 
This will allow for more validation of the included silicon projects without requring expensive equipment such as a dedicated digital logic analyzer.
The presented board acts as an interposer capable of capturing IO signals as they pass through it without interfering with them.
