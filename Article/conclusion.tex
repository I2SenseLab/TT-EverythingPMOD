\section{Conclusion}

The Everything PMOD board provides a flexible interface between the Tiny Tapeout demonstration platform and standard PMOD peripherals, reducing the effort required to prototype, debug, and validate integrated circuit designs. By combining configurable signal routing with a standardized expansion interface, the platform enables researchers to rapidly evaluate new ASIC designs using readily available laboratory hardware.

As an open-source hardware platform, the design may also be adapted for applications beyond Tiny Tapeout, including general digital signal analysis, protocol debugging, and rapid prototyping of embedded systems. Future work will expand the accompanying software, add support for additional peripheral modules, and further characterize the electrical performance of the platform under a wider range of operating conditions.
